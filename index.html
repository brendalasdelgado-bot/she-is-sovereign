The origin

I know what it costs

A few years ago I was standing in my living room, yoga mat unrolled, about to push through another practice. I was exhausted, and not the kind that sleep fixes. The kind that lives in your chest and your shoulders and behind your eyes.

And instead of pushing through, for the first time in I don't know how long, I stopped. I sat down on the floor. I asked my body, quietly, what it actually needed.

The answer was almost nothing. Very gentle movement. Long stillness. Rest. Not the practice I had planned. Not the version of me I was always performing, even for myself.

That was the moment my body started guiding my life. Not my mind. Not my fears. Not the voice that had been telling me how I should be, how much was acceptable, how to hold it all together. My body knew. It had always known. I just hadn't been taught how to listen.

I built this container because I know exactly where you are, and I know what becomes possible when a woman stops fighting her own body and starts letting it lead. This is deep, intimate work. You will not do it alone.
