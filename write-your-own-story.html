Your questions, answered

"Do I need to have attended the masterclass first?"

It is not required, but it is recommended. The masterclass gives you a felt sense of the work before you invest in the full program. If you haven't attended yet, you can register here.

"What if I can't make a live call?"

All calls are recorded and sent to you. However, the live experience — being witnessed in real time by the other women in the group — is where the deepest transformation happens. We encourage you to protect the time as a non-negotiable appointment with yourself.

"How is this different from therapy or other coaching programs?"

This is not therapy and it is not mindset coaching. This is somatic and lineage work — we work with the body and the ancestral patterns, not just the mind. You cannot think your way out of a pattern that lives in your nervous system. This goes to the root.

"How many women will be in the group?"

This is a small, intimate container. Places are limited intentionally — so every woman feels seen, held, and witnessed. This is not a large group program. It is a sacred circle.
