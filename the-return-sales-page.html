No. The Return is a somatic embodiment container, not a substitute for therapy or clinical mental health treatment. Many women do both — this work complements therapy by helping the insight actually land in the body.

This is a live container, not a digital product — once you enroll, your spot is held for you and all sales are final. Please only say yes if you are ready to fully commit to the six weeks.

I've already done a lot of therapy and inner work — will this really be different?

Most women in The Return have too. What's missing was never understanding — it's embodiment. This method meets you at the exact moment insight fails: when you're triggered, in your body, in real time.
